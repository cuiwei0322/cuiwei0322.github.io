## 刘丛菊/CongJu Liu
同济大学土木工程学院桥梁工程系博士研究生。博士研究课题是大跨度浮式桥梁台风浪流耦合环境荷载与结构响应研究，旨在通过数值模拟、风洞试验、水槽试验、风浪流耦合试验，建立结构多灾害非线性分析方法。

I am a Ph.D. Student from the Department of Bridge Engineering, Tongji University. My research focuses on investigating the coupled effects of typhoon, wave, and current environmental loads on long-span floating bridges. It aims to develop a nonlinear analysis method for assessing structural performance under multiple hazards by employing numerical simulations, wind tunnel experiments, tank tests, and coupled wind-wave-current experiments.
